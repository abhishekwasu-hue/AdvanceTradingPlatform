# Risk disclosure

Read this page before you use the platform. It explains what the platform is not, and the risks of trading.

## Paper trading only

During this phase every order, fill, position and profit or loss in the platform is simulated ("paper"). No real money moves and the platform sends no order to any broker or exchange. Simulated results are not real results: a real order can fill at a different price, fill only in part or not fill at all, and real trading carries charges, taxes and delays that a simulation can only estimate.

## Not investment advice

The platform is a software tool. It is not a SEBI-registered investment adviser or research analyst, and it offers no research-analyst service. Signals, scores, screens, backtests, AI answers and marketplace listings describe what rules and data show. They are not a recommendation to buy, sell or hold any security, and they do not predict what a price will do. Every trading decision, and its outcome, is yours alone.

## Market risk

Trading in shares, futures, options, commodities and crypto assets carries a high risk of loss. Prices can move sharply and quickly, gap past a stop-loss, or become impossible to trade. You can lose some or all of the money you put in.

Futures and options are leveraged: a small price move can cause a large loss, and some positions can lose more than the margin you paid. SEBI's studies of individual traders in equity futures and options found that most of them made net losses. Do not trade with money you cannot afford to lose.

## Backtests and past results

Backtests and past results, including simulated ones, do not show what will happen in the future. A backtest can look better than reality because of the period chosen, rules tuned to past data, missing costs or data errors. No strategy is free of risk.

## Data, technology and AI risk

- Market data can be delayed, stale, incomplete or wrong. Where it can, the platform labels where its data came from and how fresh it is, but it cannot catch every error.
- Alerts and notifications can arrive late or not at all (network, provider or device problems).
- The platform, your broker or your internet connection can fail at any time.
- AI answers can be wrong or out of date. They explain rules and data; the decisions stay with you.

## Your responsibility

Understand a product before you trade it, set your own risk limits, and check every order in your broker's app before you confirm it. If you are unsure, consult a SEBI-registered investment adviser.
