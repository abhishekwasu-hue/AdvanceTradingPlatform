M73 · ATP · Sign-in page: Google + Facebook login (owner order 1 Nov)
Finding (code main): Google sign-in exists (GS-1, auth/GoogleSignInButton.tsx) but shows only when the server has Google OAuth credentials (still not set on production) and only in sign-in mode; no Facebook. Research R35: Facebook Login for users outside the app's roles needs Advanced Access for email + public_profile (auto-approved for new Consumer apps, must be switched on, app Live) and Business Verification; an iOS app offering social login must also offer a privacy login (Sign in with Apple) — App Store 4.8.
SL-1 Provider-generic social login (OAuth2/OIDC with PKCE + state, same refresh-cookie + MFA step as GS-1): Google and Facebook now, Apple pluggable later. Buttons "Continue with Google" / "Continue with Facebook" at the TOP of both Sign in and Create account (and Join team) screens, brand-correct buttons, then "or use email". Each button appears only when its credentials are configured; admin health shows which providers are live.
SL-2 Account linking: a social login whose provider-verified email matches an existing account links to it after a password/MFA confirmation (never silent takeover); a Facebook account without an email asks for one and verifies it; users can link/unlink providers in Settings › Account (keep at least one way to sign in).
SL-3 New users via social login go through the same terms acceptance, plan limits, beta gate and 2FA rules as email sign-up; tenant creation identical.
SL-4 Security: no tokens in logs; provider tokens not stored beyond the sign-in; rate limits as /login; audit log rows for link/unlink/sign-in by provider.
SL-5 Sign-in page review on phone + desktop: the page itself — one column, buttons ≥ 48 px, clear errors in plain words, "Forgot password?" visible, no help paragraphs, brand colours; screenshots before/after.
SL-6 Owner block (one message): Google Cloud OAuth client (redirect URIs for aialgodesk.tech), Meta developer app (Consumer type, Facebook Login, Valid OAuth Redirect URIs, switch email/public_profile to Advanced Access, app Live, Business Verification when the company is registered), the env names to fill; nothing secret in the repo.
SL-7 Tests: both providers' happy path with fakes, state/PKCE mismatch, email collision → link flow, no-email Facebook, provider not configured → button hidden.
Later (not now): Sign in with Apple when an iOS app ships (required by App Store 4.8); mobile OTP login as a suggestion for the owner (needs an SMS provider + DLT registration).
