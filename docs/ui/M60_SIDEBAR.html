5. Open questions for the owner (each with a default)

  1. 1. "Analyze a chart": a Copilot tab only, or also a sidebar entry?M60 puts it inside AI Copilot; M59 CR-2 asks to put it back in the user navigation (the Markets section) and keep the standalone page. Default: a Copilot tab plus the attach button in the Ask box; /chart-analyze stays and is found by search; no separate sidebar entry (keeps one home).
  2. 2. My trades would have 5 tabs (Positions, Orders, Money, Results, Journal); the rule is at most 4. Default: 4 tabs; Journal becomes a part inside Results (Results: Summary | Journal).
  3. 3. Coach in AI Copilot or in My trades, Results? And where do Copilot Brief and Copilot Signals go? Default: Coach is a Copilot tab (as the order lists it), with a "Review with the coach" link on Results; Brief becomes "Read the chart" and its news part moves to Learn, News & events; Copilot Signals (Watchtower) opens the Signals page.
  4. 4. Admin: its own entry under ACCOUNT, or a row inside Settings? Default: its own entry at the bottom of ACCOUNT, super-admin only (the server stays the gate).
  5. 5. Where do alerts live? Today: Settings, Alerts (channels); the Notifications page (inbox); the Terminal bottom panel (Alerts tab); Find setups ("Tell me when it matches"). Default: channels in Settings, Trading, Alerts with an "Inbox" link to Notifications; the Terminal Alerts tab and the per-scan alert stay where they are.
