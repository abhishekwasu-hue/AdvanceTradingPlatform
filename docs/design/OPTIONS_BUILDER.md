# Options Strategy Builder (P1, ADR-0025)

Spec: `docs/specs/ATP_PROFITABILITY_MANUAL_TRADING_OSB_SPEC.md` §3. Build order:
- P1-a: the core port (this);
- P1-b: T+0 / IV / time, PoP, templates, selectors UI;
- P1-c: builder UI and basket.

## P1-a (built): the core port
- **Where.** `backend/app/options_builder/`, ported from Trade@73f652c:
  - `payoff.py` (Trade `strategy_payoff.py`): leg payoff at expiry, strategy curve, combined Greeks, breakevens,
    max profit / loss, default price range.
  - `templates.py`:
    - 13 ready-made structures, with the hedge listed first;
    - `READY_MADE_CATEGORIES`;
    - `hedge_first()` for baskets;
    - `build_strategy_result_from_legs`: per lot, equal lots required, positive max loss.
  - `selectors.py` (Trade `strategy.py`):
    - iron condor, with combined-PoP widening;
    - iron butterfly;
    - credit spread by PoP, at fixed strikes, or ITM;
    - naked ITM option with an optional hedge;
    - `compute_position_size`, which never rounds up.
  - `greeks.py`: leg Greeks from the platform's Black-Scholes, with IV given or solved from the premium.
- **What changed from the source.**
  - English docstrings and errors.
  - Types.
  - No NIFTY defaults: the step, hedge width and ITM depth are required, and the caller reads them from the
    instrument master.
  - Results are otherwise identical (golden set).
- **Tests** (`tests/test_p1a_options_builder.py`, 1,498 cases):
  - Golden parity: 1,451 cases generated by the Trade functions themselves, over four synthetic chains:
    - uniform PoP (Trade's own fixture);
    - logistic PoP (realistic, so the PoP selectors succeed);
    - a BANKNIFTY-like step of 100 with missing quotes;
    - a sparse chain with gaps.

    Both the success and refusal paths of every selector are covered.
  - The Trade unit tests, ported: fixed / ITM / naked selection, sizing, per-lot result, uneven lots.
  - Rules made explicit:
    - every template is hedge first;
    - `hedge_first` is stable;
    - every template's breakevens are true zero-crossings;
    - no instrument defaults;
    - a condor or butterfly whose credit covers the wings is refused (with positive controls);
    - model Greeks net like the platform's strategy Greeks.
  - Mutation checks: 13 / 13 killed (intrinsic side, breakeven at a grid zero, Greek sign, hedge order, max-loss sign,
    uneven lots, `hedge_first`, PoP threshold, condor PoP formula, ITM side, rounding of sizing, zero-risk condor and
    butterfly).
- **Regenerating the golden set.** `python tests/fixtures/options_builder/make_golden.py <trade checkout> > golden.json
  && gzip -9 golden.json`. A changed result means a behaviour change; it is either deliberate (and recorded in
  ADR-0025) or a bug.

## Open questions (provisional answers taken)
- **OB-1. The PoP definition.** The selectors use the broker's `option_greeks.pop`, Upstox's. Other brokers may not
  supply it. Provisional: P1-b computes PoP from the platform's model (lognormal, chain IV) when the broker gives none,
  and labels which one was used.
- **OB-2. The iron butterfly's PoP proxy** (from the wings' PoP) is kept as in the source, but it is an approximation.
  Provisional: kept for parity; P1-b shows a model PoP beside it.
