1. Source in the Trade repo (origin/main, read-only)
 - market_zones.py: detect_order_blocks (last opposite candle before an impulsive candle; impulsive = body ≥ impulse_mult × average range over avg_window), detect_demand_supply_zones (the tight "base" of up to base_lookback candles, each range ≤ base_tightness_mult × average range, immediately before an impulsive candle; zone = base low..high; formed at the last base candle), compute_current_role (role by current price: zone above price = resistance/supply, below = support/demand, whatever its origin label), compute_all_zones (S/R on 1H, dynamic S/R on 1M/5M/15M/30M/60M, order blocks + demand/supply on 1H, unfilled gaps on 15M), compute_intraday_sr_zones, 5m/15m confluence table.
 - signals.py: find_swings, filter_major_swings, find_support_resistance_levels (major swings clustered within a tolerance, ranked by touch count), find_order_block / is_retesting_order_block.
 - sr_dynamic.py: compute_dynamic_sr (pivot-point channel S/R: pivots over prd bars, up to maxnumpp pivots, channel width as % of range, up to maxnumsr levels, min strength).
 - level_memory.py: zone_tolerance (ATR-scaled with min/max %), merge_levels (carry levels across days, retire after N days without a touch, max distance from price, max count, role by price), apply memory to zone rows.
 - mtf_pullback_strategy.find_overnight_gaps (unfilled gaps).
 - sr_levels_v3.py: review and port its level logic too (map in TRADE_PORT_MAP.md).
2. One deliberate change (owner rule, from the later zone-lifecycle work in the Trade repo): Trade's market_zones marks a zone "FILLED" on first touch (overlap or gap-through). Do NOT port that rule as the zone's death. In ATP a zone's state is: fresh → tested (touched, reacted) → flipped (closed through and held as the opposite role) → invalidated (only when price breaks the structural swing beyond the zone). Keep Trade's "first touch" event as the transition fresh → tested, and the gap-through detection as a "tested by gap" event. Parity tests cover detection (zone boundaries, types, formation time, touch counts, dynamic S/R levels, gaps) exactly; lifecycle uses ATP's rule.
