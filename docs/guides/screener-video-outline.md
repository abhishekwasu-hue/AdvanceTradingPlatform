# Find setups - video script outline (one page)

Every scene ends on screen with: "Information and education only, not investment advice."
Use a test account and generic lists; blur or avoid any stock name next to a price. No "buy / sell / target" words.

| # | Scene | On screen | Voice-over (key line) | Guide section |
|---|-------|-----------|-----------------------|---------------|
| 1 | What a screener is | Flow diagram: list -> chart -> scan -> run -> results | "Find setups checks your list against conditions you pick, on closed candles." | 1 |
| 2 | What it does NOT do | The red box | "A match is information, not a signal and not advice. It never places an order." | 1 |
| 3 | Sign in | Sign in screen | "Sign in with email or a sign-in button." | 2.1 |
| 4 | Open Find setups | Left menu (Find > Find setups); phone bottom bar / More search | "On a phone, tap Find setups, or search 'scan' under More." | 2.2 |
| 5 | Page tour | Cards A-D and More tools | "Your list, Chart, Start a scan, Conditions, Results." | 3 |
| 6 | Pick a list | Change > Scan these stocks: Index stocks, Sectors, F&O, My watchlists, Type my own | "Up to 500 stocks; today a long list runs 50 at a time, paced." | 4 |
| 7 | Pick a chart | Chart > Daily ... 1 min; closed vs forming candle drawing | "Scans read closed candles only." | 5 |
| 8 | Start a scan | Tabs Ready scan / Template / Own words; Screen library filters | "Three templates are always there: Momentum, Oversold, Trend on the daily." | 6 |

Before recording: check which parts your organisation has switched on (guide section 14); drop scenes for parts that are off.
