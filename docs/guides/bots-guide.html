1. What a "bot" is in ATP

A trading bot is your own strategy, switched on for one symbol, that ATP checks again and again on its own. When the strategy's conditions are met on a closed candle, the bot sends one entry through the same order path that a manual order uses. Your own settings decide the size, the exits and whether it asks you first.

On the screen the menu entry is AI Trading Bot (in the BUILD section). The page itself is titled Trading bot. The bot does not think for you. It only follows the rules you picked.

The pieces

PieceWhat it is, in plain wordsWhere you see it
Strategy (the rules)The conditions that must be true on the chart for an entry, for example "a level breaks and the price comes back to it". It produces a signal, not advice.Strategies › Library / Build
Backtest (the test)The same rules run over past candles, to see how often they fired and what those trades did. It is a test of the rules, not a promise.Strategies › Test, or the Result step of Build
Deployment (the running bot)One strategy + one symbol + your choices (size, how entries are sent, Ask me first or not). Each one is a card under Your bots.AI Trading Bot page
Trading worker (the engine)A program on the ATP server that checks every running bot on a fixed rhythm, even when your browser or phone is closed.You see its result on the bot card: Checked just now
PAPER vs LIVEPAPER = practice money at real market prices; nothing reaches your broker. LIVE = real orders at your broker. Today the platform is PAPER only.Badge on each bot card and approval
Your own settingsPosition size, Risk limits, exits, holding, order type, paper capital. ATP pre-chooses none of them. Each starts as Not set.Settings › Trade settings, Settings › Risk limits
One idea to keep: the strategy gives a signal. Everything about the trade itself - lots or quantity, exits, how the order is sent, whether you approve first - comes only from your own settings. If one of them is Not set, the bot places nothing and tells you which setting it needs.

3. Step 1 - Sign in

1.1
Open the ATP website. You see the sign-in screen with the heading Sign in.
1.2
New user: press Create an account (under "New here?"). Type your Email and a Password (at least 10 characters, not a common password). If you were given a beta code, press Have a beta invite code? and type it. Accept the terms if the screen asks, then press Create account.
If the screen says "New here? Ask your team owner for an invite link.", sign-up is by invitation: ask the person who runs your team.

Connect

2.3

The daily login for orders

Some brokers end their login every morning. For Upstox the screen explains two kinds of connection:

ConnectionWhat it does (words from the screen)

For a PAPER bot: ATP needs a stored broker connection to read prices. The start check refuses a paper bot only when no broker is stored. Practise without a broker

Without a broker you can still: sign in, read Learn, browse Strategies › Library, save your Trade settings and Risk limits, and set up a strategy in Strategies › Build.

You cannot start a trading bot. The page shows A trading bot reads live prices from your broker. Connect your broker first. and the server says Connect your broker on the Broker page first: even a paper bot reads live prices through your broker.

Tests on past prices also ask for the broker: Connect your broker on the Broker page to run this on real prices. (Made-up "sample" prices are kept for the platform administrator only.)

Check: you should now see your broker listed on Broker accounts, and the Settings row in the menu without a red or amber dot.

7. Step 5 - Test first: the backtest

A backtest runs the same rules over past candles: one entry per signal bar, your exits, and Indian charges. Two places:

  • Strategies › Build, step Result: Save and test on past prices (or Test on past prices for a template).
  • Strategies › Test (page Backtesting): pick Strategy, Symbol, optional Advanced settings and Trade as (underlying or options), then Run Backtest.

What the numbers mean

NumberMeaning
Trades / Total TradesHow many entries the rules made on those candles.
Win rateShare of trades that closed with a profit.
Net P&LTotal profit or loss after the charges counted, in rupees.
Max drawdownThe deepest fall from a high point of the equity line.
Average RAverage result as a multiple of the risk per trade. Shows Needs your stop when you set no stop.
Profit factorGross profit divided by gross loss.
Avg win / Avg lossThe average winning and losing trade.
Expectancy (per trade)The average result of one trade.
EquityA small line of the account value trade by trade.

Behind tabs you see the trades list, a chart with entries and exits, and more analytics (for example most wins / losses in a row). No trades: the entry did not fire on these prices. means the rules never met.

No performance claims. The page footnote says: "Backtests use past prices; past results do not promise future ones. Paper money only." A good or bad test does not tell you what will happen. Use it to check that the rules do what you meant.
Check: you should now see Result on past prices with Net P&L, Win rate, Max drawdown, Trades, Average R and Equity, and the button Start on paper.

10. Step 8 - Pause, stop and exit

ActionWhereWhat happens
PauseBot card › PauseNo new entries while paused. State shows Paused by you. Resume starts it again.
StopBot card › Stop → Yes, stop it (or Keep it running)"It takes no new trades. An open paper trade is still watched until it closes." A stopped bot cannot be resumed; start a new one.
RemoveStopped bot › Remove (shown only when it has no open trade)"It leaves your list of bots. Its closed trades stay in your trade history…" Show stopped bots (N) lists them.
Exit a tradeThe bot card's open trade, or My trades › Positions › Exit nowCloses that paper position at the current price. Never blocked.
Exit allMy trades › Positions › Exit all (two or more positions)It asks first, with the open P&L: "Exit all N open positions at the current price? Sold legs close first, then the legs that protect them." It closes PAPER positions; a LIVE one closes in your broker's app, as its row says.
Order of safe stopping: (1) Pause or Stop the bot so it takes no new entries; (2) close any open trade with Exit now or Exit all if you want it closed now. Stopping alone does not close an open trade.

ATP also pauses a bot by itself after several failed checks in a row (the card says why), and new entries wait when a kill switch is on (section 11).

11. How a bot works after you start it (one cycle)

The trading worker runs on the server, apart from the website. Closing your browser or phone does not stop it. About once every minute (one base candle; the platform sets the exact rhythm) it does one cycle:

The main rules of the cycle

RuleIn plain words
Closed candles onlyThe decision uses closed candles. The candle still forming is dropped, the same as in the backtest.
Exits before entriesOpen positions are checked against live prices (stops, targets, your square-off time if you set one) before any new entry.
One entry per signal barThe same bar is never entered twice. If the strategy is still "in signal" on later bars, the card says "Same setup as before - already handled".
One position per botWhile its trade is open, the bot takes no new entry.
Late prices are skippedIf the candle feed is behind the clock, no signal is taken on it.
Same order pathA bot entry goes through the same checks and order record as an order you place yourself.
Your settings decideMissing Holding, New entries, size or Starting capital = nothing placed, with "Needs your setting".
Kill switchWhile a kill switch is on (whole platform, your account, or one strategy), no new entries go out. Exits still work. A risk-limit block on PAPER never turns a kill switch on.
Auto-pauseA bot that fails several checks in a row is paused, with an alert. A data problem resumes by itself when data returns.

12. PAPER first, and how LIVE is switched

Said plainly

  • Today the platform is in Phase 0: PAPER only. Every new LIVE entry is refused.
  • The LIVE switches belong to the platform owner / administrator only. A user cannot turn LIVE on from his own screens.
  • The Trading bot page has no LIVE switch at all. Strategy templates run on paper only.

Why PAPER first

  • Paper uses real market prices but practice money. Nothing reaches your broker.
  • You see how your rules, your size and your limits behave, with no money at risk.
  • You learn the screens: approvals, "Needs your setting", exits, pause and stop.

What LIVE needs (when the owner allows it later)

#Condition (from the code)Who
1The platform's PAPER-only lock is off and LIVE trading is switched onOwner / admin
2Your role has live permission and your plan includes live tradingAdmin
3Your email is verified; two-step sign-in; a fresh authenticator check when creating a LIVE botYou
4A usable broker login (the daily login for orders)You
5The go-live checklist (Settings › Advanced, shown only once the PAPER-only lock is off)You + admin

Even with "Ask me first" on a live bot, Phase 0 places an approved entry as a paper order ("Approve (paper order)").

13. Safety boxes

The AI never places orders

The AI Copilot can draft a strategy, explain a chart or propose an alert. It never places, blocks or changes an order. An order happens only when you press Place / Approve, or when your own saved bot rules do it inside the order path. A Copilot draft must be saved by you and started by you.

Exits are never blocked

No setting, limit, window or kill switch holds back a stop, a target or Exit now. Trade settings lists it as a fact: "No setting holds back a stop, a target or Exit now." Risk limits: "Exits are never checked."

Your own limits

Every Risk limit is your own switch, off by default. Turn on what you want: daily loss, max open positions, max lots, cool-downs and more. A team admin may set a ceiling above yours; then a message says Ask your admin.

Nothing is pre-chosen

ATP never fills in lots, quantity, capital, exits, order type or holding for you. Templates and the Copilot may suggest; nothing applies until you save it.

Signals, not advice

A bot's signal says that your rules were met on the chart. It is not a recommendation. Past results and paper results do not promise anything.

Short answers

  • Does the bot stop when I close the app? No. It runs on the server.
  • Can I start two bots on the same strategy and symbol? Not while one is running or paused; stop it first.
  • Does Stop close my trade? No. Close it with Exit now / Exit all.
  • Can a limit block my exit? No. Exits are never blocked.
  • Can I switch a bot to LIVE? No. LIVE is the owner's / admin's switch, and Phase 0 is PAPER only.

Points the code did not make fully clear (to confirm before the video)

  1. Python Strategy Lab: no Python strategy lab was found in the code. The only "code" path is the Pine Script translator, which makes a screen, not a bot.
  2. User kill switch button: the server has account and strategy kill switches; no button to press one was found in the user screens (the Terminal status bar shows "New orders stopped / allowed"; the admin console has the global switch).
  3. Library stop / targets: a built-in's stop and targets are labelled "Suggested — change in settings", but the three-step bot form has no field to change them; the start request carries no strategy settings. Where a user edits them for a Library bot is not clear.
  4. Paper bot and the daily login: a paper start needs only a stored broker connection; whether an Analytics-token-only connection keeps a paper bot's candles flowing all day for every broker was not proven from the code.
  5. Build test without a broker: the builder's test asks for stored history first; whether it runs with no broker for a new user was not proven.
  6. Ready-made strategy lines say "Closes before the market does", while Square-off time Not set means "ATP closes nothing by itself". Which applies to a ready-made intraday bot with no square-off saved needs confirming.
  7. Telegram: "Connect Telegram" depends on the platform's own Telegram bot token, which is an owner item.
