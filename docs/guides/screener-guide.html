1.What the screener is

The screener (the page Find setups) checks a list of stocks against conditions you pick, on closed candles, and shows which stocks meet all of them right now. You choose the list, the chart timeframe and the conditions. ATP reads the candles, works out each condition for each stock, and gives you the count and the list of matches. You can then open a match on a chart, in the same place, to see why it matched.

What happens when you press Run

What the screener does NOT do

  • It shows conditions that are true on the data shown. It is not a signal to trade and not advice.
  • It does not rank stocks as good or bad, and it makes no claim about what price does next.
  • It never places, changes or cancels an order. An alert from a scan only tells you.
  • It uses only the conditions you see. It adds no hidden rule. You can open every condition and see its settings.
  • It decides on closed candles. A candle still forming is not used by a scan.
  • The page says this in its own words under About these results: "Matches are symbols that passed the screen's filters on the data shown; they are not recommendations."

2.Before you start: sign in and open Find setups

You need an ATP account. A connected broker is not required for Daily, Weekly and Monthly scans; it is required for intraday scans (see section 9).

2.1 Sign in

  1. Open the ATP website in your browser (phone or computer). the Sign in screen with the AiAlgoDesk name. Where offered, sign-in buttons such as Continue with Google sit at the top, then or use email.
  2. Either tap a sign-in button at the top, or type your Email and Password. If two-factor sign-in is on for your account, a box Authenticator code (format 123 456).
  3. Press Sign in. the app opens on Today.
  4. No account yet? Tap Create an account under New here?. If your team uses invites, the screen says New here? Ask your team owner for an invite link.
  5. Forgot the password? Tap Forgot password?, then Send reset link.

2.2 Open Find setups

  1. On a computer: look at the menu on the left. Under the heading Find, click Find setups. the page title Find setups with the line Scan your list for the setups you pick.
  2. On a phone: tap Find setups in the bar at the bottom. If it is not there, tap More and type screener or scan in the search box; it finds Find setups.
  3. If you see Coming soon, or Find setups is not in the menu at all, the screener is not switched on for your organisation yet. Ask your administrator (section 14).
Check: you should now see the Find setups page, with two small cards at the top (Your list and Chart) and a block Start a scan.

4.Step 1 - Pick your list

The list (also called the universe) is the set of stocks the scan reads. The page opens on NIFTY 50 stocks.

  1. On the Your list card, press Change. a panel Your list and chart opens under the cards, with a box Scan these stocks.
  2. Open Scan these stocks. You can type in it to search. groups of lists: Index stocks, Sectors, F&O, My watchlists, and Other with Type my own symbols. Each list shows its size in brackets.
  3. Pick a list. To type your own, pick Type my own symbols and type the symbols in the box Your symbols (up to 500), separated by commas or new lines. under the box: N stocks · list name. If something is wrong, an amber note (see the table of notes below).
  4. Press Done. the Your list card shows the new name and the number of stocks.

5.Step 2 - Pick the chart (timeframe)

The timeframe is the size of one candle. A Daily scan reads daily candles; a 15 min scan reads 15-minute candles.

  1. On the Chart card, press Change (the same panel as the list opens).
  2. Under Chart, open the box and pick one of: 1 min, 3 min, 5 min, 15 min, 30 min, 1 hour, Daily, Weekly, Monthly.
  3. Press Done. the Chart card shows the timeframe in words, for example Daily.

Daily, Weekly, Monthly

  • Work with or without a broker (section 9).
  • Weekly and Monthly candles are built from daily candles.
  • A good place to start as a beginner: fewer candles, less noise.

1 min to 1 hour (intraday)

  • Need your own connected broker.
  • Some functions work only on some intraday timeframes (the tables in section 10 say which).
  • Session functions (opening range, previous day high) make most sense here.

Closed candle and forming candle

  • Scans use closed candles only. The page says it under the list panel: Closed bars only, read from your broker.
  • "The last bar" in this guide always means the last closed candle.
  • The only place a forming candle can be used is an alert, if you pick While the bar is forming (shown when that option is switched on; not offered for Weekly or Monthly). The app marks it "earlier, but it can change before the close". The default is When the bar closes, marked "recommended: the bar is final".
  • Inside a condition you can also read a candle further back with bars ago, and read one side on another timeframe (section 7).
Check: you should now see your timeframe in words (for example Daily) on the Chart card.

7.2 Add your first block

  1. Open Conditions with Show (if it is closed).
  2. Next to Add a condition, pick the kind of block: Indicator, Filter, Category, Rank or Group (and Formula under Advanced).
  3. Press Add a condition. a new row, for example RSI (14) above 60 for an Indicator block. Every underlined part can be changed.
  4. Tap the first word (the function) to pick another one from the list. Tap a number in brackets to change a setting; type it and press Enter (Escape cancels).
  5. Tap the comparison word (above) to change it, then the right side to change the number.
  6. To compare with another indicator instead of a number, open the row's advanced options (the small arrow at the row's end) and choose Compare with: Another indicator.

7.3 Bars ago and another timeframe

  • Timeframe chip. Each side has a small chip. Left alone it says Scan and uses the scan's chart. Pick another timeframe (for example Daily) to read that side on it. Example: the scan runs on 15 min, but close on Daily above EMA (close, 50) on Daily.
  • Bars ago. In the row's advanced options, Left side, bars ago and Right side, bars ago: a whole number from 1 to 500; blank is the last closed candle. Example: close above close 1 bar ago.
  • Some functions are read on the last bar only (structure, patterns, zones, the option chain, ranks). They take no bars-ago and no other timeframe.

8.Step 5 - Run the scan and read the results

  1. Press Run (header on a computer, bottom bar on a phone). for a larger list, Scanning X of Y stocks… with a bar and Stop. Then the results header.
  2. Read the header: Matched N of M. N is green when above 0 and amber when 0. If some stocks could not be read it adds · K could not be read, with one line giving the reason and one button (Log in, Connect broker, Retry or Try another timeframe).
  3. Read the time pill: Ran at HH:MM IST. When it turns amber it adds "x min ago, run again for newer bars". Hover (computer) to see where the data came from, for example "Data from your broker" or "Data from ATP's stored end-of-day history". Where the data badge is switched on, a badge such as EOD · date shows the session the data is from.
  4. Look at the table. By default only matches are listed; a line says how many did not match, with Show the N that did not match (or Show all N), and Matches only to go back.
  5. Press Show on chart on a matched row to open its chart in place (a window over the page; you do not leave Find setups).

8.3 After a run: keep or watch the matches

  1. Save matches as a watchlist: type a name, press Save. The list appears at once under My watchlists.
  2. Tell me when it matches (when switched on): a window says "ATP checks each time a daily bar closes for "My scan" on NIFTY 50 stocks (50 stocks), and tells you when one matches."
  3. Pick When to check: When the bar closes or (where offered) While the bar is forming. For a long list, tick Run on the whole list if you want the alert to read the whole list.
  4. Connect Telegram in the same window if you want messages there, then press Turn on the alert. "Alert on. ATP tells you when a symbol matches this scan." Under Alerts on this scan, each alert with Pause / Turn back on. The foot says: "An alert only tells you; it never places or changes an order."
A "Then" step on alerts. When your administrator has switched it on, an alert rule in Settings › Alerts can have a Then step that keeps watching a matched symbol on 5 or 15 minute candles and then sends a notification, or a PAPER (practice) proposal you approve or reject. The app says: "Setups are information for your own decision. They are not recommendations, and nothing is placed without your approval." It is not part of a normal scan.
Check: you should now see Matched N of M, the Ran at pill, the table, and (for a match) the chart window after Show on chart.

9.Where the data comes from

With your own broker connected

  • The candles come from your own broker connection. The time pill's hover says "Data from your broker".
  • ATP asks the broker in paced parts, so it stays inside the broker's request limits. A stock that waited for that limit is listed: "N stocks waited for your broker's limit. Run again in a minute to include them."
  • Your broker login ends from time to time. Then the stocks say "Your broker login has ended. Log in again to read the bars." and the button Log in opens the broker panel in place.

Without a broker: end-of-day data

  • Daily, Weekly and Monthly scans read ATP's stored end-of-day history (the exchange's daily files). The hover says "Data from ATP's stored end-of-day history". Where the data badge is on, it reads EOD · date. On other pages (charts, watchlists) the same data carries the label "Last close <date>".
  • The stored window ends at the last stored session, so the newest candle in the scan is the last closed day. Today's session is never read from this store.
  • A stock that is not in the store says "Daily history for SYMBOL is not stored yet."
  • Intraday scans (1 min to 1 hour) need your own broker: "Connect your broker for intraday bars."

Why there is no shared live data

ATP does not pass live or intraday market data from one person's broker connection to anyone else, not even from the owner or an administrator. This follows the market regulator's circular of May 2024, broker terms and the exchange's data licence. Live data for you comes only from your own connected broker. End-of-day data from the exchange's daily files is shared with everyone.

Store-first scans

When the administrator has switched it on, scans read ATP's own stored candles first:

  • Daily, Weekly, Monthly: from the stored exchange daily files only - no broker call at all.
  • Intraday: your own stored one-minute candles, built into the scan's timeframe, plus only the newest candles from your own broker, paced. An index (its volume comes from its future) and a stock the store does not hold go through your broker as before.
  • The candles are the same closed candles either way, so the same data gives the same matches.
Check: after a run, hover (or long-press) the Ran at pill: you should now see where the data came from.

Three rules that apply to every function
  • Units must match. ATP refuses to compare a price with an oscillator, for example close above RSI (14) ("compares price with index"). A plain number takes the unit of the other side.
  • Windows are whole numbers from 1 to 500 candles. A function needs enough candles before the last one; with too few, the stock says "The screen has no value on the last bar (too little history for its indicators)."
  • A setting with a fixed set of choices (for example side = up / down) must be one of those choices.

11.Three example scans, step by step

Each example uses only functions that exist in ATP. The numbers are examples of settings, not recommendations. Change them to your own.

Example 1 - A ready template: "Momentum" on NIFTY 50, Daily

  1. Open Find setups. The Your list card shows NIFTY 50 stocks (50) and Chart shows Daily. If not, set them (sections 4 and 5).
  2. In Start a scan, tap the tab Template.
  3. Tap Momentum. Conditions (2) opens with RSI (14) above 60 and close above EMA (close, 20).
  4. Press Run. Matched N of 50; the count beside each condition shows how many stayed in after it.
  5. Press Show on chart on a match. Read Why it matched: the RSI value and the close vs the average at the matched candle.
Check: you should now see two conditions, a Matched N of 50 header and, for a match, its chart window.

Example 2 - Build your own: uptrend, near support, a bullish candle

  1. Pick a list, for example F&O stocks, and the chart Daily.
  2. Tab Template → Blank: I add conditions.
  3. Beside Add a condition, pick Category, then press Add a condition. Tap the function and choose Trend; keep is; tick UPTREND. Trend (3) is UPTREND
  4. Pick Filter, press Add a condition, choose NearSupport, set tolerance to 1. NearSupport (1, 3)
  5. Pick Filter again, add, choose PatternBullish with any. PatternBullish (any)
  6. Type a name in the name box, for example Uptrend at support, press Save, then Run.
  7. If one stage leaves nothing, the note "This condition removes every symbol..." appears. Press Show the stage, then raise the tolerance or switch the block off for a moment to see which part removes the stocks.
Check: you should now see Conditions (3) under All of these must pass, a saved name, and the results header.

Example 3 - Structure with RSI divergence, and an alert

Needs "Extra screen functions" switched on (for RsiDivergence). If RsiDivergence is not in the Filter list, your app does not offer it yet.

  1. Pick a list (for example a sector) and the chart Daily. Start blank.
  2. Add a Filter: RsiDivergence with side bullish, lookback 15, degree 0. RsiDivergence (bullish, 15, 0)
  3. Add a Group. Keep Any of these. The group starts with two example blocks: remove them with the bin icon. Inside the group, pick Category as the kind of block to add and add two: StructureEvent is CHOCH_BULLISH and SwingLabel (low, 0) is HL. the group passes when either of the two is true.
  4. Add an Indicator: volume above SMA (volume, 20) (pick volume on the left, then Compare with → Another indicator → SMA with x = volume).
  5. Press Save, then Run. Open a match with Show on chart and tick Show failed conditions: in the group, the branch that was not needed is marked so.
  6. Press Tell me when it matches, keep When the bar closes, press Turn on the alert.
Check: you should now see three top-level conditions (one of them a group of two), the results, and under Alerts on this scan a line like "On · checks each time a daily bar closes · N symbols".

Find setups is missing, or shows "Coming soon".The screener is not switched on for your organisation. Ask your administrator.
"Matched 0 of 50". Is something broken?No. No stock met all conditions on the last closed candle. Look at the count beside each condition to see which one removes the most, switch it off or loosen it, and run again.
Results differ from what I see on a live chart.The scan uses closed candles; a live chart also shows the forming one. Settings (for example SMA vs EMA) may also differ.
The time pill turned amber.The results are older than two candles of your timeframe. Press Run again for newer candles.
My 200-stock list ran in parts. Why?Today one request takes up to 50 stocks, and the page paces the parts inside your broker's limits (section 4).
I have no broker. What can I scan?Daily, Weekly and Monthly, on ATP's stored end-of-day data. Intraday needs your own broker.
A function in this guide is not in my list.It needs an extra switch, or it is a Formula-only function (section 10 marks both).
Does a match mean I can take a trade?No. A match only says the conditions you chose were true on the data shown. It is information for your own study, not advice.
Can the scan place an order?No. Scans and alerts never place, change or cancel an order.

13.Glossary

WordMeaning in ATP's screener
Scan / screenA set of conditions run on a list. Both words are used.
List (universe)The stocks a scan reads.
Timeframe (chart)The size of one candle: 1 min to Monthly.
Candle / barOne period's open, high, low, close and volume. The app uses both words.
Closed candleA finished candle; it never changes. Scans use only these.
Forming candleThe current candle, still changing. Only an alert can be set to read it.
Block / stage / conditionOne line of the scan. A stage is a top-level block.
FunnelThe drawing of the conditions: each stage narrows the list.
MatchA stock for which every switched-on condition was true on the last closed candle.
Bars agoRead a value that many candles back (1 to 500).
Window (n)How many candles a function looks at.
Swing high / lowA turning point; confirmed only after price has moved away from it.
DegreeThe size of swings, 0 (smallest) to 3.
HH, HL, LH, LLHigher high, higher low, lower high, lower low: a swing compared with the one before.
BOSBreak of structure: a close beyond a swing in the direction of the trend.
CHoCHChange of character: a break against the trend.
ATRAverage true range: the usual size of a candle in price.
Median rangeThe middle candle size of recent candles: the market's own noise.
DivergencePrice and RSI make swings in opposite directions.
PCRPut-call ratio of open interest in the option chain.
Max painThe strike at which the total value of all open options would be lowest at expiry.
Open interest (OI)Derivative contracts still open.
EODEnd of day: data from the exchange's daily files.
Evidence chartThe chart that opens with Show on chart, drawing only the scan's own conditions.
PAPERPractice trading with no real money.

Remember

The screener shows conditions on data. It does not tell anyone what to do. Every match is information for your own study and decision.
