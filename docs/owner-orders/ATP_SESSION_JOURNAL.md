## Goal
The trader never writes a journal from scratch. Every trade writes its own journal entry (this already exists: Z6 / discipline_engine). Journal v2 adds the part only the trader can add (how he felt, whether he followed his plan) in about 20 seconds per trade, and turns it into short reviews and a "what I did well" list that builds confidence from evidence. Observations only, never advice (SEBI). Extend app/journal, JournalPage and the discipline_engine flag; do not rewrite them.

## What already exists (keep, do not rebuild)
Auto entry per trade (plan, fills, costs, MAE/MFE, holding time, stop history, chart reference redrawn "as of", Greeks/IV), 8 behaviour tags (SUGGESTED / CONFIRMED / DISMISSED), weekly discipline score, weekly review, `notes`, settings. Hooks in capture.py never block a trade or an exit (ADR-0004); keep that.

## J1 — Automatic capture: close the gaps
- J1-1 Broker sync: trades placed outside ATP in the connected Upstox account (manual in the broker app) appear as journal entries (source BROKER), matched to ATP trades when the same order. No duplicates.
- J1-2 Real chart picture: besides the chart reference, store a rendered snapshot (entry and exit, with the entry / exit / stop marks) as an image generated by the server from the redraw. Shown in the entry and in reviews. Store per user, delete with the account (DPDP).
- J1-3 Spread view: the legs of one spread are ONE journal entry (group_id) with combined P&L, max loss planned vs actual, credit received.
- J1-4 Plan vs actual in one line per entry: planned stop / target / size from the user's own deployment or ticket settings versus what happened. Show only differences. No default targets or R:R filters are added.

## J2 — The 20-second check-in (the human fields)
Appears on the entry the moment the trade is OPENED (push / Telegram / in-app), and again at CLOSE. Skippable; never blocks anything; never required. Fields, all optional, one tap each:
- Before entry: feeling (calm / confident / unsure / anxious / excited / fearful / FOMO — editable list), confidence 1–5.
- During: a quick note or voice-to-text note ("unease after entry") at any time.
- After close: feeling after, "Did I follow my plan?" Yes / Partly / No, and if not: mistake category (the 8 behaviour tags + "other" + user-made categories), one free line "what I would do the same / differently".
- Setup name (from the strategy / template, editable).
Rules: stored per user, private, shown only to him (never in logs, never to admin pages, excluded from analytics exports); included in account export and delete.
The auto behaviour tags and the trader's own "plan followed" answer sit side by side; a re-run never overrides his answer.

## J3 — Reviews (all generated automatically, short, in plain words)
- Daily (~5 min): after market close one card: trades, result, plan-followed count, feelings, the tags he confirmed, ONE "done well" evidence line chosen from facts (e.g. "stop kept on all trades", "waited for your own setup"). Telegram + Journal page.
- Weekly (~30 min): the existing weekly review plus: plan-followed rate, feeling vs result table (e.g. results when "anxious" vs "calm"), confidence vs result, most frequent mistake category, biggest MAE/MFE observation, one chart per notable trade. Asks him one reflection question ("what is the one thing to repeat next week?") and stores his answer.
- Monthly (~1 h): trend of the discipline score and plan-followed rate, best / worst setup by his own data, a month summary page that can be printed / exported.
- First formal review after the first 20 closed trades (earlier ones show "not enough trades yet"); never show percentages or conclusions on fewer trades than the user setting (default 20). Small samples are labelled.
- All numbers come from his own trades. No advice words (buy / sell / should / target-style). Reuse review.advice_in() guard on every generated line.

## J4 — "What I did well" proof list
- A page / card "My proof": auto-collected facts of good process, each with date, trade link and chart: stop kept, plan followed on a loss (process win even if the trade lost), waited for the setup, no revenge after a loss, exit as planned, a streak of plan-followed trades, a recovered drawdown without breaking rules.
- He can add his own entries and pin the ones that matter. Shown on Today ("your last good decision") and at the top of the Journal; also shown after a loss in a calm, factual way ("In your last 20 trades you followed the plan in N"). Facts only, no praise text generated by AI, no performance claims.
- Purpose (research-backed: mastery experiences build self-efficacy): evidence he can re-read before the market opens.

## J5 — If-then plans and process goals (his own words)
- He writes if-then plans ("If I feel unease after entry, I will check the stop and wait 5 minutes before any change"; "If a trade stops me out, I will take a short break before the next entry"). Stored as his own text; shown as a pre-market card and optionally as a reminder after a matching event (stop hit, loss). ATP never writes the plan for him and never blocks anything because of it.
- Process goals (not profit goals): e.g. "plan followed on 90 % of trades this month", set by him; progress shown in weekly / monthly reviews.

## J6 — Delivery and plans

## Compliance and safety
- Information only: no recommendations, no entry / exit prompts, no hidden rules. Feelings are the user's data; store only what he types; do not infer or label his mental health, no scores of "emotional state".
- No change to order paths, exits, or risk limits. AI (Copilot) may only summarize the journal if he asks; it never places or changes orders (ADR-0006).
- Public repo: no personal data in fixtures; tests use synthetic users.
