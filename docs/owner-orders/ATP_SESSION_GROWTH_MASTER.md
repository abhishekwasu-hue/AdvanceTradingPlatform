From Abhi — ATP MASTER ORDER 4: "READY-MADE DISH" on every page — why users come back every day and buy a plan (English)

Owner vision: a trader should open ATP and find his "lunch ready": prepared, personal, useful information, without having to dig for it. Every page must answer "what should I look at right now, and why?" before the user asks. That habit brings him back daily, and the moments where ATP saves him time or money are where he upgrades.

Research basis (record it in docs/design/GROWTH_PLAN.md):
- Indian tools charge for scans, sector rotation, F&O zones, patterns and expert-style reports (StockEdge ₹399–2,499 / month).
- Freemium converts about 2–5%; a no-card trial about 15–25%. A "reverse trial" (a permanent free tier + 14–30 days of Pro at sign-up) combines the two.
- The paywall works best at the moment the product saves real time or money.
- Engagement comes from personalised alerts, a personal news feed, onboarding prompts and native "opportunity" cards.

Rules: everything is information and education, never advice or an automatic order (SEBI; the RA question stays in the compliance pack, and public "setups" stay labelled educational). PAPER-only Phase 0 unchanged. No dark patterns. NEVER reward trade count or activity: reward discipline (following one's own plan), so the habit we build is a healthy one. Apply this order AFTER the Master Order 3 audit has cleaned the pages, and design both together. English only; self-review; tests; screenshots desktop + phone, light + dark.

PART 1 — THE DAILY RHYTHM (the core habit; personalised by the user's watchlists, positions and settings)
- Pre-market brief (before the open; in-app + Telegram / WhatsApp / push, per the user's choice):
 - global cues, GIFT NIFTY, FII / DII, VIX;
 - today's events (results, RBI, expiry);
 - the structure of NIFTY / BANKNIFTY with today's key areas;
 - the 5 setups worth watching from the user's own lists;
 - his open positions' risk for the day.
- Mid-session pulse (one short push, optional): what changed (a key level broken, OI shift, the sector leader / laggard, a position near its invalidation).
- Closing wrap (after the close): how the market moved and why (sectors, breadth, OI); his P&L and his discipline score for the day; "what to watch tomorrow".
- Weekly review (weekend): the week's market story; his performance and behaviour patterns; the coming week's events and expiries.
- Each digest is one screen, scannable in 30 seconds; it opens straight to the related page.

PART 2 — PAGE BY PAGE (for each page: the ready dish shown on open, the reason to return, and the paid hook)
1. Dashboard ("Today"):
 - Dish: the brief card, market breadth, the sector heatmap, his positions with today's risk, the top setups from his lists, events today.
 - Return: it changes through the day.
 - Paid: more lists covered, intraday refresh.
2. Broker: one-tap connection health. Dish: "connected, data live". Proactive: renewal reminders before expiry.
3. Charts:
 - Dish: when a chart opens, the reader's areas / lines / channel / patterns drawn automatically (the toggle on by default for paid, a preview for free), with a one-line "story" of the chart and the setup state.
 - Plus: multi-timeframe strip; "similar past setups on this chart and what followed" (from the replay data).
 - Paid: the full overlay, unlimited analyses.
4. Option chain:
 - Dish: "what the chain shows" in 3–5 sentences; support / resistance from OI; the build-up; the expected move; the IV state; the reader's areas against the strikes.
 - Return: intraday OI shifts with alerts ("PE writing moved up to 22,800").
 - Paid: the Sensibull-style charts history, Ask AI, saved analyses.
5. Find setups (screener) and Custom scan:
 - Dish: "Today's setups" ready-made, grouped by method (pullback to demand, sweep and reclaim, range edges, divergence), each with a mini chart and the reason; sector-filtered; ranked by evidence count.
 - Return: new matches arrive during the session.
 - Paid: all methods, intraday scans, alerts, unlimited custom scans.
6. Sector & index view (new, from the default watchlists):
 - Dish: sector rotation (leaders / laggards over day / week / month), the index heatmap, each sector's strongest and weakest stocks, relative strength vs NIFTY.
 - Paid: industry-level rotation, history.
7. Strategy library / Build / Test:
 - Dish: ready, explained strategies (including the credit-spread styles) with honest backtests, "works best when …" in plain words, and one-tap "paper-trade this".
 - Paid: unlimited builds and backtests, the optimizer, walk-forward.
8. AI Copilot:
 - Dish: the 8 starter jobs (Master Copilot order), answers grounded in data, the morning brief inside the chat.
 - Paid: more AI requests, deep analyses; BYOK free path.
9. Autopilot / Signals:
 - Dish: each signal with its entry model, evidence, invalidation and "Trade this setup" (a basket from his settings); the per-model PAPER statistics ("E3 signals: 62% reached the next area over 3 months").
 - Paid: more deployments, more symbols, Telegram approval.
10. Positions / Orders / Portfolio / Risk:
 - Dish: the premium capture % per short-premium position; the structure alerts; the adjustment comparison; risk in rupees and plain words; event risk ahead.
 - Paid: adjustments, the portfolio risk view.
11. Performance / Journal:
 - Dish: an automatic journal with entry / exit chart snapshots; "your best and worst setups"; behaviour patterns (moving the SL, overtrading after a loss); the discipline score trend; a monthly report PDF.
 - Return: the weekly review.
 - Paid: the full analytics, PDF reports.
12. News & events / Company research:
 - Dish: news filtered to his watchlists and positions, each with "why it matters" in one line; the results calendar for his stocks; a one-screen company snapshot (business, key numbers, promoter / FII changes, upcoming events).

13. Plan an options trade (Options Builder):

14. Coach and guide:

15. Notifications:
