# Mission

ATP exists to help retail traders who lose money become consistently disciplined, and through that, to give them a
real chance at profitability.

**No guarantee is made or implied anywhere in the product.** Regulator studies show that most retail F&O traders lose
money, and the product says so plainly.

Every feature is judged by one question. Does it reduce the behaviours that lose money:
- oversizing;
- trading with no stop;
- revenge trading and overtrading;
- trading without a plan;
- ignoring costs and events?

Or does it increase the behaviours that make money:
- setups based on an edge;
- risk control;
- review;
- patience?

ATP is a tool and an education platform. It never recommends securities. Exits are never blocked (ADR-0004), and the
AI acts only on a person's approval (ADR-0006).
